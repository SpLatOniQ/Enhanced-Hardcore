# Adds a way to revive players in hardcore... 
<sub>Yeah, that's pretty much it</sub>

Making a whole new server just because one of your friends died in hardcore is **really annoying**. 

Which is why I made this mod! Now you can easily *(or not, depending on your choice of revival item)* revive your friends just by **right-clicking their grave / corpse** with an item.

---

## Configuration

You can fully customize the mod to your liking by editing the config options:

* `enableRespawningAtGrave` — Toggles whether the revived player spawns at their set respawn point or directly at their grave.
* `revivalItem` — Changes the required revival item to any item you want.
